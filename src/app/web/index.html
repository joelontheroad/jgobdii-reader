Automotive Bus Topologies & Baud Rates

Standard protocols and pin assignments across manufacturers:

  • High-Speed CAN (HS-CAN): 500 kbps (Pins 6 & 14) — Engine, Transmission, ABS/Stability.
  • Commercial / Heavy Duty: 250 kbps (SAE J1939) — Medium/Heavy Duty Trucks & Marine.
  • Ford Medium-Speed CAN (MS-CAN): 125 kbps (Pins 3 & 11) — Instrument Cluster, HVAC, Body.
  • GM Single-Wire CAN (GMLAN / SWCAN): 33.3 kbps (Pin 1) — Infotainment, Doors, Lighting.

Distinguishes federal emissions mandates from manufacturer proprietary codes:

DigitClassificationApplication & Meaning

1Manufacturer SpecificProprietary code defined by the vehicle maker for specific hardware/features.

💡 Diagnostic Best Practice: When an ECM reports both generic (e.g. P0300) and manufacturer-specific codes (e.g. P1300), always resolve the manufacturer-specific codes first. OEM codes often pinpoint the precise component failure (such as an ignition coil igniter feedback wire) that caused the generic fault.

⚠️ Confirm Mode 04 DTC Clear

Critical Diagnostic Directive:

Executing OBD-II Mode 04 will instruct all connected vehicle Electronic Control Units to:

  • Turn OFF the Check Engine Malfunction Indicator Lamp (MIL).
  • Erase all confirmed (Mode 03) and pending (Mode 07) trouble codes.
  • Purge all Mode 02 Freeze Frame telemetry snapshots.
  • Reset all 11 I/M readiness drive-cycle monitor flags to "Not Ready" (the vehicle will not pass emissions test until a full drive cycle is completed).
